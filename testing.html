Testing and render times

Measured on real computers with the current release of THETA Render.

These are the times THETA Render needed to produce one image on the graphics cards I have been able to test. All times are averages, in seconds.

Graphics cardSmallabout 1.0 megapixelMediumabout 1.5 megapixelsLargeabout 2.1 megapixels
Laptop: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060, 6 GB16.2 s28.8 s54.0 s
Desktop: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070, 12 GB4.2 s8.6 s12.4 s
Desktop: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080, 16 GB3.0 s4.8 s8.0 s
Desktop: AMD Radeon RX 7900 XT, 20 GB6.3 s17.2 s29.5 s

Notes

Small, Medium and Large are the three output sizes you choose in the app.

Times depend on the graphics card, its driver, the image and what else the computer is doing. The first render after starting the app takes longer, because the models have to load.

The lowest specification tested is a laptop with an RTX 2060 and 6 GB of video memory. RTX cards with 4 GB may work but have not been tested.

I cannot test every graphics card, which is why a free trial is offered: you can check on your own computer, with your own images, before you buy.
